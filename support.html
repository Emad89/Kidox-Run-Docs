Kidox Run — Support

A multiplication game for children aged about 6 to 10. Swipe into the lane with the right answer and keep running.

Getting help

Email unicornsoftsolution@gmail.com with the question, the device you are using, and the version of the app (shown on the main menu). Most messages are answered within a couple of days.

Common questions

How do I change what my child is practising?
Tap Math Run on the main menu. The setup screen that appears sets the operation (multiply, add, subtract or divide), which times tables are in play, how large the numbers get, and how fast the run moves.
The run is too fast for my child.
On the same setup screen, lower "Starting pace" and set "Gets faster" to its lowest setting. Nothing is timed, so a slower run simply gives more thinking time.
How does playing with the camera work?
Choose "Move my body" on the setup screen, prop the device up, and stand back so the whole upper body is in frame. Lean left or right to change lane. Good lighting helps. This is optional — swiping always works.
Do I have to allow camera access?
No. The camera is only used for the optional movement controls, and the game is fully playable without it. Nothing is ever recorded or sent anywhere — see the privacy policy.
Does it need an internet connection?
No. Kidox Run works entirely offline and never connects to the internet.
Are there ads or in-app purchases?
No. There is no advertising, nothing to buy inside the app, and no subscription.
How do I reset progress?
Deleting and reinstalling the app clears all saved progress and settings.
